# v0.3.1 release and final check — 2026-09-02

## Fix

Production v0.3.0 exposed a harmless but misleading startup race: the first loopback health request could reach port 8080 before the restarted service had bound it, causing raw `curl: (7)` stderr even though a later bounded retry succeeded and the Installer correctly committed.

The inline fix at `scripts/installer/40-transaction.sh::wait_for_health` redirects each curl attempt's stdout and stderr to `/dev/null`. It does not change the ten attempts, two-second connect/max bounds, one-second inter-attempt delay, success predicate, final `Service health check failed: <url>` diagnostic, or transaction rollback propagation. The generated `scripts/install.sh` was regenerated.

The mock curl now emits recognizable health-failure stderr. Regression assertions prove:

- one failed attempt followed by success performs two calls, exposes no raw curl stderr, and commits;
- ten failed attempts expose no raw curl stderr, retain the Installer-owned final diagnostic, and execute the existing rollback path.
